About This Guide

Who this guide is for, what you need, and how to read the steps.

Welcome to the Prince Alex Digital School Management System — a secure, cloud-based platform that lets your school manage students, teachers, classes, attendance, fees, exams, results and parent communication from one dashboard. This guide is written for the people who will use the system every day: school owners, head teachers, secretaries, bursars and class teachers.

What You Need Before You Begin

  • A device — computer, tablet or smartphone with a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  • Internet connection — the system runs in the cloud, so your data is safe even if the device changes.
  • Login credentials — your school's email address and password, issued during onboarding by Prince Alex Digital.
  • Your school details — logo, motto, address, phone numbers and fee structures, ready for first-time setup.

Getting Started

Logging in, finding your way around the dashboard, and logging out safely.

2.1 Logging In

  1. Open the system

    Open your browser and go to your school's system address (provided during onboarding). You will land on the welcome page.

  2. Open the login form

    Click the Login button at the top of the page (or the graduation-cap logo).

  3. Enter your credentials

    Type the email address and password issued to your school, then click Login.

  4. Land on your dashboard

    You are taken straight to the Dashboard — your school's private workspace.

Forgot your password?On the login screen, open the reset-password link and follow the instructions sent to your email. Still stuck? Call or WhatsApp 0717 384 875.

2.2 Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard is your school at a glance. It updates automatically as staff work:

CardWhat It Shows
Total StudentsNumber of students on roll.
Total TeachersNumber of teachers in the faculty list.
Total CollectedAll fee payments received (KES).
Total ExpectedFees due according to your fee structures.
Outstanding BalanceWhat is still owed (Expected − Collected).
ClassesNumber of classes/grades created.
Recent AnnouncementsNotices posted in the school and by Prince Alex Digital.
Attendance SummaryToday's Present / Late / Absent / Excused breakdown.

2.3 Navigating the Sidebar

All modules live in the left sidebar. Click any item to open it — the page title at the top changes with you. On a phone, tap the bars icon to slide the menu open. The menu is grouped:

  • Core: Dashboard, Students, Teachers, Classes & Subjects, Timetable, Attendance
  • Finance: Fee Structure, Fees Management, Fee Summary
  • Academics: Exams & Results, Announcements, Library, Transport, Hostel, Inventory
  • School Operations: Academic Years & Terms, Streams, Subjects, Teacher Assignments
  • Administration: School Admins, Roles & Permissions, Activity Logs, Settings
Why can't I see every menu item?Menus depend on the permissions granted to your account (see Section 11). Teachers only see the classes assigned to them.

2.4 Logging Out

  1. Open the account menu

    Click your profile avatar at the top-right of the screen.

  2. Click Logout

    Always log out when leaving a shared computer — especially in a busy school office.

First-Time Setup

Do these nine things once, in order, and the rest of the system will run itself. Only the School Owner account can complete every step.

Step 1 — Configure Your School Settings

SidebarSettings
  1. Fill in the School Profile

    Upload your school logo, then enter the school name, motto, address, phone contacts, current term and academic year, and the Principal / Head Teacher's name. These details appear on report cards and fee statements.

  2. Choose how admission numbers are created

    Under Admission Number Configuration, pick Automatically generated (e.g. ABC/2026/0001 using your own prefix) or Entered manually. Numbers are kept unique within your school either way.

  3. Set up marks & grades

    Tick Use letter grades (A–E) if you want letter grades on results, then set the minimum mark for each grade (e.g. A from 80). Leave it unticked if your school uses marks only.

  4. Save

    Click Save Settings.

Step 2 — Create the Academic Year & Terms

SidebarAcademic Years & Terms
  1. Add the academic year

    Click + Add Academic Year, enter the year with its start and end dates.

  2. Add the terms

    Click + Add Term for each term (e.g. Term 1, Term 2, Term 3) with start and end dates.

  3. Mark the active year and term

    Use the Active toggle on the row of the current year and term. Attendance, fees and exams all follow the active term.

Only one active at a timeKeep exactly one academic year active and one term active per year. When a new term begins, come back here and switch the active term before taking attendance or fees.

Step 3 — Add Subjects (Learning Areas)

SidebarSubjects
  1. Open Subjects and click + Add Subject

    Enter the subject name and code.

  2. Select applicable grades

    Choose which classes/grades the subject is taught in. Repeat for every learning area in your curriculum (CBC/CBE learning areas are fully supported).

Step 4 — Create Streams (Optional)

SidebarStreams
  1. Click + Add Stream

    If a grade is large enough to split into groups (e.g. Grade 7 → East / West / North), create each stream, pick its parent grade/class, assign a class teacher and set the capacity.

Step 5 — Create Classes & Map Subject Teachers

SidebarClasses & Subjects
  1. Click + Create Class

    Name the class (e.g. Grade 7), choose the class teacher, and map each subject to the teacher who will teach and assess it. The mapping drives exam entry and timetables later.

Step 6 — Add Your Teachers

SidebarTeachers
  1. Click + Add Teacher

    Enter the teacher's ID, full name, subjects taught and phone number. The table shows each teacher's current teaching workload automatically.

  2. Edit or remove as staff change

    Use the actions on each row of the Faculty Management table to keep records up to date.

Step 7 — Assign Teachers to Classes

SidebarTeacher Assignments+ Assign Teacher
  1. Pick the teacher, grade, stream and subject

    Each assignment links one teacher to one subject in one class/stream.

  2. Save

    The assignment list fills up — this is what controls which classes a teacher can see and mark in the system.

Limited visibility by designA teacher sees only the classes, streams and subjects they are assigned to — unless you grant them wider access in Roles & Permissions (Section 11).

Step 8 — Set Fee Structures

SidebarFee Structure+ Set Structure
  1. Choose class, term and year

    Every fee structure is tied to a specific class in a specific term/year.

  2. Enter tuition and other fees

    Type the Tuition amount plus any Other Fees (lunch, activity, transport etc.). The total is calculated for you.

  3. Repeat per class per term

    Set a structure for every class before the term starts so expected fees are always correct.

Step 9 — Add School Admins & Permissions

SidebarSchool Admins
  1. Click + Add School Admin

    Create an account for each staff member who needs access (secretary, bursar, deputy…). They log in with their own email and password.

  2. Open Roles & Permissions

    Select an administrator and tick exactly the modules they may use. The School Owner always keeps full access.

You're ready!Once these nine steps are done, your day-to-day work is just Sections 4–8 below: students, attendance, fees, exams and communication.

Managing Students

Admit new students, import last term's register in bulk, keep records current, and export your data any time.

4.1 Admitting a New Student

SidebarStudents+ Add Student
  1. Open the admission form

    In the Student Directory, click + Add Student.

  2. Capture the student's details

    Upload a passport photo, then enter full name, gender, grade/class and stream, academic year, and the guardian's name and phone contact.

  3. Check the admission number

    If your school uses auto-generation it is filled for you; in manual mode the Auto-fill button suggests the next free number. Numbers are always unique.

  4. Save the record

    The student now appears in the directory with their photo, status and guardian contact.

4.2 Importing Students in Bulk

SidebarStudents Download Template
  1. Download the official template

    Click Download Template to get the import spreadsheet with the correct column headings.

  2. Fill in your students

    Add one row per student — names, gender, class, stream, guardian contacts. Do not change the column headings.

  3. Upload and confirm

    Click Import, attach your file, review the preview, then click Confirm Import. All students are added at once.

4.3 Finding & Filtering Students

The Student Directory search bar accepts a name or admission number. Combine it with the dropdowns to filter by Grade/Class, Stream, Status (Active, Inactive, Transferred, Graduated, Left School, Archived) and Academic Year. Click Reset to clear all filters.

4.4 Updating a Student Through Their Profile

  1. Open the student

    Click the student's row in the directory to open their full profile.

  2. Use the profile actions

    Edit updates personal details · Promote moves them to the next class at year end · Transfer records a move between classes/streams · Archive safely retires a record without deleting history.

Your data is yoursUse Export CSV on the Students page to download the whole directory for backups or board reports. The same export buttons exist on Attendance, Fee Summary, Timetable and Activity Logs.

Daily Attendance

Marking a class takes under a minute — and re-marking the same day simply updates the record instead of duplicating it.

5.1 Marking Today's Attendance

SidebarAttendance
  1. Select the class and date

    Choose the class, confirm the date (defaults to today), then click Mark Attendance.

  2. Mark each student

    Set every student to Present, Late, Absent or Excused.

  3. Use quick actions to go faster

    Tap All Present to mark everyone present in one click, then adjust only the exceptions. Clear resets the form.

  4. Save

    Click Save Attendance. A live summary shows the Present / Late / Absent / Excused counts as you work.

5.2 Reviewing & Correcting History

The Attendance Records & History table lists every marked day per class. Click any record to reopen and correct that day — saving overwrites the old marks cleanly, never creating duplicates.

End-of-term registersClick Export CSV on the history table for a complete register — no more manual mark books.
Records in the wrong term?Attendance is stored under the active term. If something looks off, check the active term in Academic Years & Terms first.

Fees & Payments

Record every payment once and the balances, statements and collection reports build themselves.

6.1 Recording a Fee Payment

SidebarFees ManagementRecord Payment
  1. Open the payment form

    In Fees Management, click Record Payment.

  2. Select the student

    Search by name or admission number and pick the student from the results.

  3. Enter the payment details

    Choose the term and year the payment is for, type the amount paid (KES), select the method (cash, bank, M-Pesa…), and add the date and any note/receipt number.

  4. Save

    The payment appears instantly in the Fee Records table with its expected / paid / balance figures, and updates the dashboard totals.

6.2 Checking a Balance & Printing a Statement

Fees Management Check Student Balance
  1. Look up the student

    Click Check Student Balance, search and select the student, then pick the academic term and year.

  2. View the fee statement

    Click View Fee Statement to open a full statement: expected fees, every payment made, and the running balance.

  3. Print or share it

    Click Print Statement for a parent-ready copy on your school's letterhead details.

6.3 Collection Reports for Management

SidebarFee SummaryLoad Summary
  1. Filter by class, term and year

    Pick your filters and load the summary.

  2. Read the numbers

    Each row shows Expected vs Collected vs Outstanding plus a Collection % per class.

  3. Export for meetings

    Click Export CSV to take the report to the board or owner.

No structure, no balanceIf a student's balance shows zero unexpectedly, check that the Fee Structure exists for that class, term and year (Section 3, Step 8).

Exams & Report Cards

Enter subject marks, review results per student, and send beautifully personalised term newsletters to every parent.

7.1 Entering Exam Marks

SidebarExams & Results+ Add Exam Record
  1. Create the exam entry

    Name the exam (e.g. Opener, Mid-Term, End-Term), pick the class/stream, student and date.

  2. Type the marks per subject

    A field is provided for each subject that student takes. Totals, averages and letter grades calculate automatically using your Settings ranges.

  3. Save the record

    Results are grouped by Exam + Student, so each student shows as a single row with all their subject marks inline.

  4. Find results quickly later

    Use the search box plus the All Exams / All Classes filters above the results table.

7.2 Generating Bulk Term Newsletters (Report Cards)

SidebarExams & Results Bulk Term Reports
  1. Open the generator

    Click Bulk Term Reports.

  2. Fill in the term details

    Select the term and year, then enter the term opening date, closing date, next-term opening date, a short Term Highlights summary, the Principal's message and the principal's name for the signature.

  3. Choose who receives it

    Pick the Class Filter (all classes or one) and the Performance Filter: All Students, Top Performers, or Needs Improvement.

  4. Generate in your format

    Generate Term Newsletter (PDF) for printable report cards · WhatsApp Messages for copy-ready parent texts · or the email option.

What parents receiveEvery newsletter is fully personalised: the student's progress, subject marks, attendance, fee status, term highlights and the principal's message — branded with your school's details from Settings.
Delete carefullyDeleting an exam group removes all its marks and cannot be undone — export a CSV backup first if unsure.

Other Modules

Four more toolkits that come standard with your system.

Library

Add Book builds your catalogue (title, author, category, copies, shelf). Issue Book lends a copy to a student with a due date; the Borrowing Ledger tracks what is out and overdue. Copies available decrease and restore automatically.

Transport

Add Vehicle registers each bus with its route, driver and capacity. Assign Student places learners on routes; the table shows how full each vehicle is at a glance.

Hostel

Register dormitories/rooms and allocate boarding students to beds so occupancy is always known.

Inventory / Store

+ Add Item stocks the store (category, unit, minimum level). Stock In / Out records every movement with a note; items falling below their minimum are flagged so you reorder in time. A movements log keeps the full history.

Use only what you needThese modules are independent — a day school can ignore Hostel and Transport entirely without affecting anything else.

Users & Permissions

Give every staff member exactly the access they need — nothing more, nothing less.

The Three Kinds of Users

  • School Owner — full control of your school: settings, money, users and permissions. There is one owner account per school.
  • School Admin — created by the owner for office staff. Access is limited to the modules you tick.
  • Teacher — sees only the classes, streams and subjects assigned via Teacher Assignments.

Granting Module Permissions to an Admin

SidebarRoles & Permissions
  1. Select the administrator

    Choose them from the dropdown at the top of the page.

  2. Tick their modules

    The permission editor lists every module — e.g. give a bursar Fees Management + Fee Summary but not Exams.

  3. Save

    Their sidebar updates the next time they open the system.

Managing & Auditing Accounts

In School Admins you can see each admin's role, account status, creation date and last login — and suspend or remove accounts the same day staff leave.

In Activity Logs, every critical operation is recorded (who did what, when, on which record). Click Export CSV when you need a formal audit trail.

Security habitAudit accounts once a term. Fewer active accounts with narrow permissions = fewer accidents.

Best Practices

Habits that keep your school's data clean all year.

  • Switch the active term on day one of every new term before marking attendance or fees.
  • Set fee structures before opening day, so expected balances are right from the first payment.
  • Mark attendance daily by 9 a.m. — absentees can be followed up while it still matters.
  • Record payments the same day they are received; statements are then always accurate.
  • Keep guardian phone numbers updated — WhatsApp broadcasts only reach numbers saved on the student's record.
  • Export CSVs monthly (Students, Fees, Attendance) as an offline backup for your records.
  • Promote students once, at year end, using each student profile's Promote action.
  • Archive rather than delete student records, and remove leavers' staff accounts immediately.
  • One person per account — never share the owner password; give staff their own admin accounts.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Fast answers to the questions we hear most.

SituationWhat To Do
I forgot my passwordUse the reset link on the login screen and follow the email instructions. If it doesn't arrive, check spam, ask the School Owner, or call us.
A teacher can't see their classCheck Teacher Assignments — teachers see only assigned grades/streams/subjects. Add the missing assignment.
A menu is missing for an adminAn owner needs to tick that module in Roles & Permissions.
Balances show zero / wrong expected feesThe Fee Structure for that class-term-year is missing or wrong. Fix it under Fee Structure.
Attendance saved into the wrong termThe active term had been switched late. Correct the active term, then re-mark the affected days.
Duplicate admission number errorThe number already exists in your school. Search the directory first; auto mode prevents this entirely.
Bulk import failedColumn headings were changed or a required field is empty. Re-download a fresh template and compare.
Wrong payment recordedEdit or reverse it from the Fee Records table so records stay honest — don't ignore it.
WhatsApp links not generated for some parentsThose guardians have no phone number saved. Edit the student profile and add the contact.
System feels slow / won't loadRefresh the page (F5), check your internet, or try another browser. Your data is safe in the cloud meanwhile.
We lost a device with accessLog that account out / change its password from School Admins, then re-secure the device.
