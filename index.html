Platform Features

Everything your school runs on. In one system.

Stop juggling registers, receipt books and spreadsheets. Prince Alex Digital brings every office task into one clean, secure dashboard.

Student Records

Full student profiles, admission numbers, classes, guardians and contacts — searchable in seconds and exportable to CSV.

Teacher Accounts

Every teacher gets their own login and role, with exactly the right access to students, classes and the data they need.

Classes & Subjects

Set up classes, streams and subjects to match your school layout, then organise students and teachers under each class.

Attendance Tracking

Take daily attendance in a few clicks and keep a live attendance summary for every class — no more paper registers.

Fee Structures

Define fee structures per class and term exactly the way your fee ledger works: tuition, transport, lunch and more.

Fees Management

Record payments in KES and watch collected, expected and outstanding balances update automatically on your dashboard.

Exams & Results

Record exams and marks, keep a term-by-term results history and have every student's performance in one place.

Announcements

Reports & Exports

Export student lists, fee balances and reports to CSV in one click — perfect for Excel, archives and audits.

Secure Cloud Data

Hosted on Google’s Firebase with fast, secure authentication. Access from anywhere — always backed up for you.

Works Everywhere

Responsive design that works on PC, tablet and smartphone. No installation and no technical team required.

Owner Control

Running more than one school? Manage every school, admin and teacher role from a single master control panel.

Example: A from 80, B from 70, C from 60, D from 50 → 70–79 is a B, below 50 is an E.
